Questions

How long until I can sit on it?

Usually a few hours. Airflow speeds it up, so open a window or run a fan or the AC after we leave.

Will every stain come out?

Most food, drink, dirt and pet stains lift out. Old stains that have set, ink, bleach and dye damage may only lighten. We'll tell you before we start.

Is it safe for kids and pets?

We clean mainly with hot water and rinse out our cleaning solution, so very little residue is left behind.

Do I need to be home?

Someone needs to let us in and show us the pieces. We need a regular outlet and a spot near the couch to work.
